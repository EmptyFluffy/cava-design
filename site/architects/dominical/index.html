(The year)

The dry season runs from January to March: 3 months under 60 mm of rain. The wettest month is October, with 719 mm.

Afternoons are hottest in March, at 29 °C on average, and coolest in November, at 27 °C. Nights stay between 23 and 24 °C.

In the dry season the afternoon wind comes from the southwest. Rooms that open to it on one side and away from it on the other cool themselves.

(The sun)

The sun here is never far from overhead. At noon it stands 80° high in March and September and 57° in December, and from April 15 to August 30 it passes north of overhead, so walls and glass that face north take sun as well.

What heats a house is the low afternoon sun. It sets at 294°, west-northwest, in June and at 246°, west-southwest, in December. That side needs the deepest roof.

Sunrise falls between 5:14 and 5:57 all year, and sunset between 17:12 and 17:60.

(Building here)

  1. (01)

    Land use certificate

    The uso de suelo, from the Municipalidad de Osa: what the zoning allows on the lot.

  2. (02)

    Water availability letter

    From the local ASADA or from AyA. Without it there is no building permit, so it is worth asking for before buying.

  3. (03)

    Drawings at the CFIA

    Filed through the CFIA's APC platform and signed by the architect in charge.

  4. (04)

    Building permit

    Issued by the Municipalidad de Osa.

Within 200 m of the ordinary high-tide line the maritime zone law (Ley 6043) applies: the first 50 m are public and cannot be built on, and the next 150 m are held under a concession from the municipality, not owned. Check where a lot sits before you buy.

(Questions)

When is the best time to build in Dominical?

The dry season, from January to March, when each month gets less than 60 mm of rain. Earthworks, foundations and the roof go faster then, so it helps to have the permit in hand before January.

Who issues building permits in Dominical?

The Municipalidad de Osa. The drawings are filed first with the CFIA, through its APC platform, and the permit comes after the land use certificate and the water availability letter.

Can I build near the beach in Dominical?

Not in the first 50 m from the ordinary high-tide line, which are public. The next 150 m are held under a concession from the municipality rather than owned, with their own rules. Beyond 200 m, a titled lot follows the normal permit process.

How hot does it get in Dominical?

Afternoons average 29 °C in March, the hottest month, and 27 °C in November. Nights stay between 23 and 24 °C. Shade on the west side and air moving through the rooms do more for comfort than anything else.

How far is Dominical from the airports?

By road, without traffic, 5 h 35 min from Daniel Oduber airport in Liberia (302 km) and 3 h 40 min from Juan Santamaría airport in San José (190 km).
