(The year)

The dry season runs from December to March: 4 months under 60 mm of rain. The wettest month is September, with 385 mm.

July rains less than June and August: the short break known as the veranillo de San Juan.

Afternoons are hottest in March, at 29 °C on average, and coolest in November, at 25 °C. Nights stay between 17 and 18 °C.

In the dry season the afternoon wind comes from the northeast. Rooms that open to it on one side and away from it on the other cool themselves.

(The sun)

The sun here is never far from overhead. At noon it stands 80° high in March and September and 57° in December, and from April 17 to August 28 it passes north of overhead, so walls and glass that face north take sun as well.

What heats a house is the low afternoon sun. It sets at 294°, west-northwest, in June and at 246°, west-southwest, in December. That side needs the deepest roof.

Sunrise falls between 5:14 and 5:59 all year, and sunset between 17:12 and 18:02.

(Questions)

When is the best time to build in Santa Ana?

The dry season, from December to March, when each month gets less than 60 mm of rain. Earthworks, foundations and the roof go faster then, so it helps to have the permit in hand before December.

Who issues building permits in Santa Ana?

The Municipalidad de Santa Ana. The drawings are filed first with the CFIA, through its APC platform, and the permit comes after the land use certificate and the water availability letter.

How hot does it get in Santa Ana?

Afternoons average 29 °C in March, the hottest month, and 25 °C in November. Nights stay between 17 and 18 °C. Shade on the west side and air moving through the rooms do more for comfort than anything else.

How far is Santa Ana from the airports?

By road, without traffic, 3 h 40 min from Daniel Oduber airport in Liberia (205 km) and 25 min from Juan Santamaría airport in San José (13 km).
