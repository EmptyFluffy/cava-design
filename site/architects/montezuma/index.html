(The year in Montezuma)

The dry season runs from December to March: 4 months under 60 mm of rain. The wettest month is September, with 551 mm.

Afternoons are hottest in February, at 30 °C on average, and coolest in October, at 27 °C. Nights stay between 24 and 25 °C.

Best time to start*

The best time to start building here is from November to March. Those 5 months bring 340 mm of rain in total; the other 7 bring 2,565 mm, 8 times as much.

Earthworks, foundations and concrete pours need dry ground, and many roads to the lots only stay passable without rain. On a day with 10 mm of rain or more, that work usually stops.

A 4-month phase of earthworks, foundations and structure that starts in November meets about 6 such days here; the same phase started in April meets about 28. Starting in November saves about 21 days of stopped work: some 3 weeks of crew and machinery paid to wait, and the pumping and drainage that wet excavations need. So the permit should be ready by October. Still, it is a recommendation, not a rule: building goes on through the rains, more slowly, and no project should sit for months waiting for the dry season.

(The wind in Montezuma)

In the dry season the afternoon wind at Puntarenas comes from the south.

Rooms that open to the wind on one side and away from it on the other cool themselves, and a roof that sheds the rain lets them stay open in the wet months too.

(The sun in Montezuma)

The sun here is never far from overhead. At noon it stands 80° high in March and September and 57° in December, and from April 16 to August 28 it passes north of overhead, so walls and glass that face north take sun as well.

What heats a house is the low afternoon sun. It sets at 294°, west-northwest, in June and at 246°, west-southwest, in December. That side needs the deepest roof.

Sunrise falls between 5:18 and 6:02 all year, and sunset between 17:16 and 18:05.

The ground here gets 2,000 kWh of sunlight per m² a year, and solar panels make about 1,625 kWh a year for each kW installed.

(Questions about building in Montezuma)

Where is Studio CAVA based, and how do you work in Montezuma?

We work from San José and Guanacaste. We visit Montezuma for the analysis of the lot and, during construction, for the supervision visits; the rest happens by video and email, in English or Spanish.

Can you design my house in Montezuma if I live abroad?

Yes. The first call is free and by video, and each stage of the design ends with drawings you review and approve at a distance. We walk the lot in Montezuma ourselves, and during construction we send a report with photos after each visit.

How much does it cost to build a house in Montezuma?

A 250 m² house with a pool comes to about US$733,000 to US$1,130,000 in Montezuma, with a high finish, design, permits and VAT. Our cost estimator gives the range for your own size, finish and lot, with the schedule month by month.

What should I check before buying a lot in Montezuma?

The land use certificate from the Concejo Municipal de Distrito de Cóbano, which sets the use, setbacks, coverage and height; water availability in writing from AyA, the local ASADA or a well; the setbacks from any river or spring; whether the lot is within 200 m of the high-tide line, where the maritime zone law applies; and the slope. Our lot study checks it in 2 to 4 weeks, before you sign.

When is the best time to start building in Montezuma?

Ideally from November to March, so that the earthworks, foundations and structure fall in the dry season. A 4-month phase of that work started in November loses about 6 days to heavy rain, against about 28 if it starts in April, about 21 days of crew and machinery. It is a recommendation, not a rule: building goes on through the rains, more slowly, and no project should sit for months waiting for the dry season. To start then, have the permit ready by October.

Who issues building permits in Montezuma?

The Concejo Municipal de Distrito de Cóbano. The drawings are filed first with the CFIA, through its APC platform, and the permit comes after the land use certificate and the water availability letter.

Can I build near the beach in Montezuma?

Not in the first 50 m from the ordinary high-tide line, which are public. The next 150 m are held under a concession from the municipality rather than owned, with their own rules. Beyond 200 m, a titled lot follows the normal permit process.

How hot does it get in Montezuma?

Afternoons average 30 °C in February, the hottest month, and 27 °C in October. Nights stay between 24 and 25 °C. Shade on the west side and air moving through the rooms do more for comfort than anything else.

How far is Montezuma from the airports?

By road, without traffic, 3 h 20 min from Daniel Oduber airport in Liberia (170 km) and 5 h 25 min from Juan Santamaría airport in San José (267 km).
