(Our services)

One studio, from the lot to the keys.

A project usually takes them in this order, and each one can also be hired on its own.

All services →
  1. (01) Lot study Before you buy: what the rules, the water and the slope let you build on a lot, and roughly what it costs. 2 to 4 weeks · Fixed fee→
  2. (02) Master planning For land that will hold more than one house: where the road, the lots, the houses and the shared buildings go, decided after reading the land. 6 to 12 weeks · Fixed fee→
  3. (03) Architecture Houses, rental villas and small hotels, from the first sketch on the lot to the drawings the permit and the builder need. About 5 months for 250 m² · CFIA: from 6% of the works→
  4. (04) Permits We file the project with the CFIA and the municipality, and answer every observation until the licence is issued. About 3 months · Included when we design→
  5. (05) Interiors Kitchens, bathrooms, built-in furniture, lighting and finishes, designed with the house or for a remodel. With the architecture, or 2 to 4 months · Agreed fee→
  6. (06) Construction supervision Weekly site visits, the site logbook, payments checked against work done, and a report with photos after every visit. The length of the works · CFIA: 3% or 5% of the works→
  7. (07) Sustainable design Houses and hotels designed to a sustainability standard, with RESET or LEED® certification if you want the label. Alongside the design and the build · Separate, agreed up front→
