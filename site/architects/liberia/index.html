(The year)

The dry season runs from December to April: 5 months under 60 mm of rain. The wettest month is September, with 363 mm.

July rains less than June and August: the short break known as the veranillo de San Juan.

Afternoons are hottest in April, at 35 °C on average, and coolest in October, at 29 °C. Nights stay between 23 and 24 °C.

In the dry season the afternoon wind comes from the east. Rooms that open to it on one side and away from it on the other cool themselves.

(The sun)

The sun here is never far from overhead. At noon it stands 79° high in March and September and 56° in December, and from April 19 to August 26 it passes north of overhead, so walls and glass that face north take sun as well.

What heats a house is the low afternoon sun. It sets at 294°, west-northwest, in June and at 246°, west-southwest, in December. That side needs the deepest roof.

Sunrise falls between 5:18 and 6:05 all year, and sunset between 17:16 and 18:08.

    From the local ASADA or from AyA. Without it there is no building permit, so it is worth asking for before buying.

    Filed through the CFIA's APC platform and signed by the architect in charge.

(Questions)

When is the best time to build in Liberia?

The dry season, from December to April, when each month gets less than 60 mm of rain. Earthworks, foundations and the roof go faster then, so it helps to have the permit in hand before December.

Who issues building permits in Liberia?

The Municipalidad de Liberia. The drawings are filed first with the CFIA, through its APC platform, and the permit comes after the land use certificate and the water availability letter.

How hot does it get in Liberia?

Afternoons average 35 °C in April, the hottest month, and 29 °C in October. Nights stay between 23 and 24 °C. Shade on the west side and air moving through the rooms do more for comfort than anything else.

How far is Liberia from the airports?

By road, without traffic, 15 min from Daniel Oduber airport in Liberia (13 km) and 3 h 35 min from Juan Santamaría airport in San José (196 km).
