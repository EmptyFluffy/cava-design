(How it runs)

  1. (01)

    Preliminary studies

    2 to 3 weeks

    CFIA: Estudios preliminares, 0.5%

    We write the brief with you, read the rules of the lot and consult the institutions.

    You receive

    • The programme: rooms, areas and how you will use the house
    • The rules and limits of the lot
    • The technical studies the project needs
  2. (02)

    Concept

    About 1 month

    CFIA: Part of the anteproyecto

    Two or three ways to place the house on the lot, tested against the sun, the wind and the views.

    You receive

    • Plans and sections of each option
    • Images of the house in its place
    • A first cost range
  3. (03)

    Anteproyecto

    1 to 1.5 months

    CFIA: Anteproyecto, 1%

    The option you choose, developed with the structure and the services in mind.

    You receive

    • Floor plans, sections, elevations, roof plan and site plan
    • The structural and services approach
    • Images of the main spaces
    • A preliminary cost estimate
  4. (04)

    Construction drawings

    2 to 2.5 months for 250 m²

    CFIA: Planos y especificaciones, 4%

    The full set, coordinated with the structural, electrical and mechanical engineers 2.

    You receive

    • Location, site and levels
    • Floor plans, sections and elevations
    • Storm and waste water
    • Foundations, structure and details, backed by calculations
    • Roof plan
    • Electrical and telecoms, plumbing and mechanical
    • Construction details and specifications
  5. (05)

    Cost estimate

    With the drawings

    CFIA: Estimación global de costos, 0.5%

    Quantities taken from the drawings and priced at market rates.

    You receive

    • A cost estimate to read the builders' quotes against

(Fees)

Four services are compulsory in every building project in Costa Rica: preliminary studies, anteproyecto, construction drawings and a global cost estimate 2. Their minimum fees add up to 6% of the estimated cost of the works: 0.5%, 1%, 4% and 0.5% 1. The Constitutional Court made those minimums binding again in 2024 3, and the estimate they are applied to cannot be lower than the CFIA's own valuation of the works.

Each stage is paid before it starts. The drawings are adjusted to the builder's contract if it is signed within six months, a remodel pays one and a half times the rates, and professional services carry 13% VAT 4. Asking for more options, or reopening a phase you approved, is billed as extra 2.

(Questions)

What do architects charge in Costa Rica?

At least 6% of the cost of the works to design a new building: 0.5% for preliminary studies, 1% for the anteproyecto, 4% for the construction drawings and 0.5% for the cost estimate, plus 13% VAT 1,4. Supervising the works is separate, 3% or 5%. These minimums are mandatory again since a ruling of the Constitutional Court in September 2024 3.

What exactly do I receive with the drawings?

The set the CFIA requires: location and levels, floor plans, sections and elevations, storm and waste water, foundations, structural plans and details, roof plan, electrical and telecoms, plumbing and mechanical, and construction details, backed by the engineers' calculations and with the specifications on the sheets 2.

How many changes are included?

Two rounds of changes in each design phase. A phase counts as approved if you do not object within ten working days of receiving it; asking for more options, or reopening a phase you approved, is billed as extra under the CFIA rules 2.

How long from the first meeting to the permit?

For a 250 m² house, about five months of design and about three of permits. SETENA, a condominium committee or the ICT add time, and municipalities differ: in 2025 the CFIA measured an average of about 10 days to approve in Liberia and Santa Cruz, and 35 in Quepos 5.

Do I need to be in Costa Rica?

No. Most of the design runs by video, with a visit to the lot at the start and on site whenever a decision needs the place.

How are the fees paid?

Before each stage, on the estimated cost of the works. The drawings are adjusted to the builder's contract, and supervision to the final cost of the works, extras included 1.
