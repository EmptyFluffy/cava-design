(Services)

Ourservices

From the study of a lot before you buy it to the last visit to the works: what each service includes, what you receive, how long it takes and how it is charged.

  1. (01)Lot study2 to 4 weeks Before you buy: what the rules, the water and the slope let you build on a lot, and roughly what it costs. Fixed fee
  2. (02)ArchitectureAbout 5 months for 250 m² Houses, rental villas and small hotels, from the first sketch on the lot to the drawings the permit and the builder need. CFIA: from 6% of the works
  3. (03)PermitsAbout 3 months We file the project with the CFIA and the municipality, and answer every observation until the licence is issued. Included when we design
  4. (04)InteriorsWith the architecture, or 2 to 4 months Kitchens, bathrooms, built-in furniture, lighting and finishes, designed with the house or for a remodel. Agreed fee
  5. (05)Construction supervisionThe length of the works Weekly site visits, the site logbook, payments checked against work done, and a report with photos after every visit. CFIA: 3% or 5% of the works

(In order)

A project usually takes them in this order: a study of the lot before buying, the design with its interiors, the permits, and supervision while it is built. Each one can also be hired on its own.
